Status: PROPOSED ONLY; NOT PREREGISTERED FOR EXECUTION; no corpus accessed in this phase.

## Choice between two admissible directions
A. Zodiac visual-label anchor: independent iconographic identifications, then blind test whether labels predict icon classes on distinct non-protected folios. Greater semantic discrimination but needs independently sourced iconographic ground truth and sufficient distinct non-protected folios.
B. Botanical root/leaf morphology: blind iconographic classifications and label association on distinct non-protected folios. Potentially more folios, but much higher identification ambiguity and observer degrees of freedom.
Select A conditionally for lower iconographic ambiguity. Do not claim any zodiac sign identification until separately documented and sourced.

## Rival hypotheses
H1: labels encode or index iconographically identified zodiac categories; a predeclared label feature predicts independently assigned icon classes on unseen eligible folios.
H0: labels follow local layout/scribe/section conventions; conditional permutation of labels within the same visual layout/scribe strata predicts equally well.
H2: repetition is driven by generic production templates, not iconographic identity.
H3: apparent matches are post hoc transcription/segmentation artifacts.

## Positive and negative observations
Support H1 only if preregistered out-of-folio predictive gain exceeds the 95th percentile of a predeclared stratified permutation null AND survives independent transcription and an adversarial layout-only rival, with multiplicity correction.
Weaken H1 if gain is nonpositive, unstable across transcription, or indistinguishable from null/rival.
A significant structural association alone does not establish translation or identify a word.
