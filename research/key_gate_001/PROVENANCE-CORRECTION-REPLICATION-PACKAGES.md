# Critical provenance correction: do not conflate replication packages

Date: 2026-11-15
Status: VERIFIED FROM PUBLIC PROJECT PAGES

A previous project note incorrectly described the 226-file / 3.0 MB package as the Caspari-Faccini evaluation package. This is not supported by the public documentation.

The Voynich Studies "Data & code" page describes its package as containing scripts/reports, normalized corpora, zodiac-label dataset, claim registries for the site's two papers, and run_all.sh. The papers are:
- "A Workshop Cipher"
- "Thirty Names per Sign"

The publications page further describes later versions (v1.1/v1.2) and their DOI records. These are the site's own research replication artifacts, not, on the evidence inspected, a standalone archive of the Caspari-Faccini falsification experiment.

The Caspari-Faccini hypothesis page separately states that scripts and data for its evaluation are open in a replication package, but the exact archive URL, version, contents, manifest, and file-level link to the reported 17.5%, 69.7%, 67.9%, and 90.3% figures have NOT yet been independently identified in this audit.

Therefore:
- Do NOT claim the 226-file/43.3 MB package is the Caspari evaluation package.
- Do NOT claim Caspari numerical results have been reproduced from that package.
- The reported numerical evaluation remains an external published result, not a local reproduction.
- The exact Caspari evaluation package and its artifact-to-claim mapping remain TO BE LOCATED.
- No protected Voynich holdout has been accessed.
