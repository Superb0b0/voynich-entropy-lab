An endpoint directly imposed, used for fitting, candidate selection, tuning or stopping is not independent explanatory evidence for that candidate. An endpoint that is a mathematical consequence of a fitted constraint must likewise be identified. Known exploratory findings cannot become blind discoveries by relabeling; use prospective predictions on permitted evaluation partitions and disclose previous exposure. Use common evaluation endpoints not directly encoded or fitted by any compared candidate wherever possible. Where none exist, do not claim a fair explanatory comparison. Match geometry and nuisance constraints across mechanisms. Distinguish evidence for reproduction, predictive generalization, and mechanism discrimination. Include a baseline and an explicitly engineered positive control, labeled as such. Consider mechanism ablation and predictions that differ between families; specify them prospectively.
